About ARLO

ARLO is a student-focused AI assistant designed to help with everyday questions, college FAQs, calculations, study support, notes and planning.

Features

  • AI chat and study support
  • FAQ and college information lookup
  • Calculator and date/time responses
  • Notes and planner tools
  • Dark and light mode

Technology used

Flask, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and local FAQ-based study logic.

Privacy note

ARLO works without any external AI account or paid API. Everything runs locally on the server.
